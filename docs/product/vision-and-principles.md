# Vision and principles

Status: canonical | Owner: product | IDs: `PRD-001`–`PRD-006` | Last verified: 2026-08-09

- `PRD-001` One lifecycle: a CFP submission can become reviewed content, a managed speaker, a scheduled session, and a public listing without re-entry.
- `PRD-002` Role clarity: organizers, reviewers, speakers, and attendees see purpose-built surfaces and only authorized data.
- `PRD-003` Explain state: status, next action, validation failure, delivery failure, and integration state are visible.
- `PRD-004` Human control: AI suggestions are optional and always have deterministic manual alternatives.
- `PRD-005` Reproducible demo: seeded data, fake providers, and reset make every evaluated journey repeatable.
- `PRD-006` Accessible publishing: public and embedded views expose only explicitly published data.

Success means the journeys in [personas and journeys](personas-and-journeys.md) pass end-to-end and the [scorecard](../quality/scorecard.md) has no P0 gap.
